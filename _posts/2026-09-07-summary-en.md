### [China Approves Aipusiwei, Its First AI-Assisted Original Drug](https://www.gelonghui.com/live/2653282) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Westlake University, Westlake Laboratory, and Westlake Pharmaceutical \(Hangzhou\) Co., Ltd. jointly developed the innovative drug Yisitewei Hydrochloride Tablets \(brand name: Aipusiwei\), which has received conditional approval from China&\#x27;s National Medical Products Administration \(NMPA\) for treating mild-to-moderate COVID-19 in adults. The drug is described as China&\#x27;s first AI-assisted developed original drug, taking only about three and a half years from initial discovery to completion of clinical trials. The approval marks a notable milestone for AI-driven drug discovery in China&\#x27;s pharmaceutical sector, though it is conditional and specific to a COVID-19 indication. The announcement offers no technical detail on the AI methods, models, or validation processes involved in the drug&\#x27;s development.

telegram · zaihuapd · Sep 6, 09:10

**「Background」** China&\#x27;s drug registration classification places Class 1 innovative drugs at the highest tier, reserved for originally discovered novel medicines, and press reports describe this compound as Westlake University&\#x27;s first fully self-developed Class 1 drug. Conditional approval by the National Medical Products Administration is an accelerated regulatory pathway that allows a drug to reach the market before all confirmatory evidence is complete, generally with post-marketing obligations attached. The milestone sits within the broader field of AI-driven drug discovery, where machine learning is used to speed up target identification, molecular screening, and lead optimization during early development.

**「Impact」** The conditional NMPA approval gives Chinese adults with mild-to-moderate COVID-19 a domestically developed treatment option and establishes a regulatory precedent for AI-assisted drug discovery in China, potentially encouraging other developers to pursue similar AI-driven pipelines. However, the approval is conditional and tied to a COVID-19 indication, so its broader effect on AI drug development standards remains to be seen.

telegram · zaihuapd · Sep 6, 06:43

**「Background」** CXMT is China&\#x27;s largest maker of DRAM—the memory chips used in servers and PCs—and focuses solely on DRAM rather than also producing NAND flash like rivals SK Hynix. **「Why it matters」** As Samsung, SK hynix, and Micron shift capacity toward high-margin HBM for AI, CXMT&\#x27;s expansion into commodity DRAM could ease DDR5 price pressures for PC builders and data-center buyers by late 2026.
